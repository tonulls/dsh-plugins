# `/ecc-carrier-relationship-management`

Freight carrier portfolio management practices: FMCSA discovery and verification, rate and fuel surcharge negotiations, RFPs and routing guides, scorecards, volume allocation, and relationship renewals for truckload, LTL, and intermodal.

## Short Request

```text
/ecc-carrier-relationship-management Create a scorecard structure for evaluating our LTL carriers on timeliness, claims, cost, and data quality.
```

## Template

```text
/ecc-carrier-relationship-management Help with [onboarding / RFP / negotiation / evaluation / allocation / renewal] for a [truckload/LTL/intermodal] carrier. Lanes/regions: [data], volume: [period and range], service constraints: [conditions]. Separate confirmed data from assumptions; do not invent quotes or FMCSA status.
```

## Step-by-Step Request

```text
/ecc-carrier-relationship-management Prepare a plan for [RFP/portfolio review/renewal] for [network, regions, transport modes] during [dates]. Goals: [savings/reliability/capacity], constraints: [service, insurance, procurement policy].
1. Segment needs by lanes, volume, and truckload/LTL/intermodal modes; list missing data.
2. For new candidates, define a carrier and FMCSA verification process; treat current status as unconfirmed unless a source and date are available.
3. Create a comparable RFP structure: rate, fuel surcharge, accessorials, terms, and validity period; do not invent supplier offers.
4. Propose a scorecard with metric definitions and cadence (for example, service, claims, costs, and variance); explain how results affect allocation and the routing guide.
5. Prepare negotiation priorities and renewal options, identifying decisions that require procurement approval.

Format: missing data; carrier/criteria matrix; risks and next-step plan. Do not enter agreements, change allocations, or contact carriers without explicit approval.
```
