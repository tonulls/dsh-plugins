# ecc-investor-outreach

**Command:** `/ecc-investor-outreach`

**Purpose:** Prepare cold emails, warm-introduction copy, follow-ups, updates, and investor communications for fundraising. Use for concise, personalized messages to angels, VCs, strategic investors, and accelerators.

## Prompts

### 1. Short Request
> /ecc-investor-outreach Write a short email to an investor about [company].

### 2. Template with Context
> /ecc-investor-outreach Investor: [name/fund], stage: [round], context: [warm introduction/first contact], substantiated points: [data]. Do not invent relationships or metrics.

### 3. Detailed Request
> /ecc-investor-outreach Draft a personalized [cold email/introduction/follow-up] to investor [profile] about [company]. Use only supplied facts, tone [tone], length up to [limit]. Prepare subject and body; do not send or search online without approval. Mark details requiring confirmation.
