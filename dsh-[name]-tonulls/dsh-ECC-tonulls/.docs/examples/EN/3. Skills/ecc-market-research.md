# `/ecc-market-research`

**Purpose:** Conducts market research, competitive analysis, investment screening, and industry analysis, citing sources and producing decision-oriented conclusions. Use to estimate market size, compare competitors, study funds and technologies, or research business decisions.

## Short Prompt
```text
/ecc-market-research Assess the [segment] market for [decision] and distinguish verified information from estimates.
```

## Template Prompt
```text
/ecc-market-research
Context: [project path / snippet / system / symptom].
Task: research the market and competitive landscape for a business question concerning [goal or subject].
Focus: sources, market size/segments, competitor comparisons, assumptions, and verifiable conclusions.
Constraints: Separate verified facts from estimates and cite sources; conduct external research only if available and authorized.
Output format: [brief conclusion, evidence, priorities, and next steps].
```

## Detailed Prompt
```text
/ecc-market-research
Perform the task “research the market and competitive landscape for a business question” for [project/component/scenario].
1. Record baseline information, scope, and success criteria; list missing information.
2. Study only relevant context and assess sources, market size/segments, competitor comparisons, assumptions, and verifiable conclusions.
3. Form conclusions and next steps without applying changes or presenting assumptions as facts.
4. Compare results with criteria, cite evidence, and distinguish confirmed conclusions from assumptions.

Constraints: Separate verified facts from estimates and cite sources; conduct external research only if available and authorized.
Include outcome, important findings/actions, unverified areas, risks, and safe next steps.
```
