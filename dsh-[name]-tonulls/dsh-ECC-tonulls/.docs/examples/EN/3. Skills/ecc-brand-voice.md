# `/ecc-brand-voice`

Derives a style profile from real writing samples—posts, essays, launch notes, documentation, or a website—and applies it to content, emails, and social media without generic “AI clichés.”

## Short Request

```text
/ecc-brand-voice Derive the style from the attached posts and rewrite the launch announcement in that voice, not as generic AI copy.
```

## Template

```text
/ecc-brand-voice Use these style sources: [texts/files]. Build a profile from observable features—vocabulary, rhythm, tone, structure, distinctive techniques, and what to avoid. Then adapt [material] for [channel and audience]. Do not reuse facts from the samples unless they apply to the new material.
```

## Step-by-Step Request

```text
/ecc-brand-voice Create a reproducible voice profile for [author/brand] based on [list of real texts]. Prepare [email/post/page] for [audience, channel, purpose].
1. Check the samples for sufficiency and variety; note their genres and possible tone differences.
2. Extract a profile with examples of observable traits: sentence length, vocabulary, formality, rhythm, humor, structure, and directness. Separate confirmed observations from assumptions.
3. Record clichés and undesirable traits to avoid. Do not imitate a personality beyond the supplied sources.
4. Prepare content for the specified channel, preserving source facts and length requirements [limit].
5. Briefly explain how it matches the profile and list areas where information was insufficient.

Format: style profile; finished text; brief self-check. Do not invent facts or publish/send the text without separate approval.
```
