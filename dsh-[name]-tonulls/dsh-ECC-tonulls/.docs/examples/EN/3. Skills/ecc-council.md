# `/ecc-council`

Facilitates discussion of an ambiguous choice through four distinct perspectives to surface trade-offs and support a reasoned decision.

## Simple Request

```text
/ecc-council Help me choose between a monolith and splitting the service into two components.
```

## Template Request

```text
/ecc-council
Decision: [question]
Options: [A, B, others]
Criteria and constraints: [timeline, budget, risks]
Context: [facts]
Facilitate a structured discussion of different positions and provide a conditional recommendation.
```

## Detailed Request

```text
/ecc-council I need to decide: [question]. Consider options [list] in context [facts], with constraints [timeline/cost/security/reversibility]. Have four voices separately present arguments for each path, risks, and challenging objections; then compare them against the criteria, identify facts and assumptions, and formulate a decision or conditions under which the preference would change. Take no actions and change no systems: analysis only. End with a brief decision, open questions, and safe next checks.
```
