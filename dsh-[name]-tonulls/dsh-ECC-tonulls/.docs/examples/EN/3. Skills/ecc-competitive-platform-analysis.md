# `/ecc-competitive-platform-analysis`

Defines the boundaries of the competitive landscape: who counts as a competitor, which tier they belong to, and what sources to research. This is the classification stage before comparative evaluation.

## Simple Request

```text
/ecc-competitive-platform-analysis Identify the main competitors for our task management service for small distributed teams.
```

## Template Request

```text
/ecc-competitive-platform-analysis
Product: [description and category]
Target customers: [segment and geography]
Market: [boundaries]
Known alternatives: [list]
Define inclusion criteria, assign tiers, and recommend sources. Do not present unverified assumptions as facts.
```

## Detailed Request

```text
/ecc-competitive-platform-analysis Scope the competitive landscape for [product] in market [geography/category]. First define what counts as a competitor and the selection criteria; then classify candidates as direct, adjacent, or alternative solutions, state the rationale for each, and recommend sources for further research. Identify gaps and disputed inclusions separately. Do not proceed to scoring or strategic recommendations—that is the next stage. Use only available materials; do not conduct web searches without approval. Result: sample boundaries, candidate table with tier and rationale, sources, and questions to verify.
```
