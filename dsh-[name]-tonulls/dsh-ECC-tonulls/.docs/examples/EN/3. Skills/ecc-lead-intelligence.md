# ecc-lead-intelligence

**Command:** `/ecc-lead-intelligence`

**Purpose:** Finds and qualifies valuable contacts using signals, fit ranking, warm-path discovery, and tailored outreach drafts for email, LinkedIn, and X based on the user's actual style.

## Prompts

### 1. Short Request
> /ecc-lead-intelligence Help evaluate these prospects against [criteria] and suggest a personalized warm first contact; do not send messages.

### 2. Template with Placeholders
> /ecc-lead-intelligence I am seeking contacts for [product/offer] in [industry, role, region]. Priority signals: [events/needs], exclusions: [criteria]. Use only supplied sources [list], rank by justified fit, and identify gaps. Draft for [email/LinkedIn/X] in the style of these examples [materials]. Do not send anything or make unsupported personal inferences.

### 3. Detailed Phased Request
> /ecc-lead-intelligence Build a research list of leads for [offer] and target profile [ICP]. 1) Record inclusion/exclusion criteria and required signal recency. 2) For each contact, distinguish confirmed facts from assumptions and cite source/date/context when available. 3) Score fit and relative priority using transparent criteria; flag insufficient data rather than guessing. 4) If text examples are supplied, identify observable voice traits and prepare separate short drafts for email, LinkedIn, and X with a relevant warm opener. Format: contact table with rationale and sources, followed by drafts and unknowns. Do not search online, use connected services, or send/publish messages without separate approval.
