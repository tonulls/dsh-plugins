# `/ecc-marketing-campaign`

**Purpose:** Plans and runs marketing campaigns: audience research, positioning, campaign concept, landing-page copy, emails, posts, ads, short video scripts, and content plans. Use to coordinate a multi-channel product launch or prepare marketing materials.

## Short Prompt
```text
/ecc-marketing-campaign Create a campaign plan for launching [product] on [channels], including audience, messaging, and metrics.
```

## Template Prompt
```text
/ecc-marketing-campaign
Context: [project path / snippet / system / symptom].
Task: plan a multi-channel product launch campaign for [goal or subject].
Focus: audience, positioning, goal, assets, channels, calendar, and measurable metrics.
Constraints: Provide recommendations; do not apply changes, send messages, or publish.
Output format: [brief conclusion, evidence, priorities, and next steps].
```

## Detailed Prompt
```text
/ecc-marketing-campaign
Perform the task “plan a multi-channel product launch campaign” for [project/component/scenario].
1. Record baseline information, scope, and success criteria; list missing information.
2. Study only relevant context and assess audience, positioning, goal, assets, channels, calendar, and measurable metrics.
3. Form conclusions and next steps without applying changes or presenting assumptions as facts.
4. Compare results with criteria, cite evidence, and distinguish confirmed conclusions from assumptions.

Constraints: Provide recommendations; do not apply changes, send messages, or publish.
Include outcome, important findings/actions, unverified areas, risks, and safe next steps.
```
