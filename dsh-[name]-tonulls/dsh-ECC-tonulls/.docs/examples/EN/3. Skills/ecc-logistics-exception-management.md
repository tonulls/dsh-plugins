# `/ecc-logistics-exception-management`

**Purpose:** Freight exception management practices: delays, damage, loss, shortages, and carrier disputes. Covers escalation, carrier and mode considerations, claims, and choosing between compensation and dispute. Use for delivery issues, claims, and carrier disputes.

## Short Prompt
```text
/ecc-logistics-exception-management Prepare a plan to handle a freight delay and damage, identifying required evidence, escalation, and next deadline.
```

## Template Prompt
```text
/ecc-logistics-exception-management
Context: [project path / snippet / system / symptom].
Task: prepare a freight exception resolution plan for [goal or subject].
Focus: delay/damage/loss, evidence, escalation, carrier SLA, and claim options.
Constraints: Provide recommendations; do not apply changes, send messages, or publish.
Output format: [brief conclusion, evidence, priorities, and next steps].
```

## Detailed Prompt
```text
/ecc-logistics-exception-management
Perform the task “prepare a freight exception resolution plan” for [project/component/scenario].
1. Record baseline information, scope, and success criteria; list missing information.
2. Study only relevant context and assess delay/damage/loss, evidence, escalation, carrier SLA, and claim options.
3. Form conclusions and next steps without applying changes or presenting assumptions as facts.
4. Compare results with criteria, cite evidence, and distinguish confirmed conclusions from assumptions.

Constraints: Provide recommendations; do not apply changes, send messages, or publish.
Include outcome, important findings/actions, unverified areas, risks, and safe next steps.
```
