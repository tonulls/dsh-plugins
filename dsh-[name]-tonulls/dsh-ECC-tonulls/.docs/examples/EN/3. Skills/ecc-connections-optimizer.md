# `/ecc-connections-optimizer`

Helps review X and LinkedIn contact networks: recommends who to remove/follow and drafts personalized warm outreach messages. Account changes require separate approval.

## Simple Request

```text
/ecc-connections-optimizer Help identify which contacts in my LinkedIn network are most useful for the topic of sustainable energy.
```

## Template Request

```text
/ecc-connections-optimizer
Channel: [X/LinkedIn]
My current goals: [topics or audience]
Available contact list: [data without unnecessary personal information]
Examples of my style: [texts]
Provide prioritization recommendations and draft messages; do not unfollow or send anything.
```

## Detailed Request

```text
/ecc-connections-optimizer
Help reorganize my professional network around goal [goal] and channel [X/LinkedIn]. Use only supplied or explicitly available data. First define relevance criteria, then group contacts by signals and uncertainty, prepare candidates to keep/remove, and suggest new types of contacts to find. Draft several personalized messages in my style based on examples [examples]. Do not follow, unfollow, like, message, or take any other account action; before any such action, request separate explicit approval for the specific action and recipient. Result: justified recommendations, uncertain cases, and unsent drafts.
```
