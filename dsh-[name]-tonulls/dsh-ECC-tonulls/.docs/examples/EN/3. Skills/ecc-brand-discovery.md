# `/ecc-brand-discovery`

A structured interview for creating or reimagining a brand: eight modules covering purpose, positioning, audience, personality, voice, narrative, and founder tension. The interview can span multiple sessions; answers can be saved and synthesized into `90_SYNTHESIS.md`.

## Short Request

```text
/ecc-brand-discovery Interview me to clarify the positioning of a new studio brand and prepare a foundation for its brand book.
```

## Template

```text
/ecc-brand-discovery Help me [create/reimagine] brand [name/project] for [market/audience]. Start with [module or a fresh interview], ask one substantive question at a time, and deepen answers with follow-up questions. Keep my wording separate from your conclusions; if working in a project, suggest a place for notes before writing files.
```

## Step-by-Step Request

```text
/ecc-brand-discovery Conduct a multi-stage brand interview for [name/project]. Goal: [new brand / repositioning / brief for designers or writers].
1. Check whether answers have already been saved; when continuing, do not repeat confirmed questions and identify incomplete modules.
2. Work through the modules: purpose, positioning, audience, personality, voice, narrative, and founder tension (plus other planned interview topics). Ask follow-ups such as “why?” and use projective questions, but do not replace answers with your own assumptions.
3. At the end of each stage, briefly reflect what you heard and ask me to confirm or correct questionable conclusions. Do not write materials to disk without an agreed location.
4. After the agreed modules are complete, prepare a synthesis in `90_SYNTHESIS.md`, separating facts and quotes from interpretations.

Result: completed/open modules, confirmed brand language, contradictions, and final brand book. If information is insufficient, ask the next question instead of filling gaps with invented facts.
```
