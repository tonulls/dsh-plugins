# `/ecc-prediction-market-oracle-research`

**Purpose:** Research prediction markets as data sources or oracle signals for products, agents, dashboards, and enterprise analytics. Provides source-based analysis of implied probabilities, caveats, and integration options without investment recommendations. Use to evaluate such sources or signals.

## Short Prompt
```text
/ecc-prediction-market-oracle-research Assess whether [prediction market] is a suitable signal source for [product].
```

## Template Prompt
```text
/ecc-prediction-market-oracle-research
Context: [product question, markets of interest, available sources, and required time horizon].
Task: research prediction markets as a data source or product signal for [target component/outcome].
Focus: sources and quote quality, market-implied probability, liquidity/biases, integration limitations, and no investment recommendations.
Constraints: Use substantiated sources, distinguish facts from estimates, do not provide investment advice or take external action without approval.
Format: [brief result, evidence/rationale, risks, and next step].
```

## Detailed Prompt
```text
/ecc-prediction-market-oracle-research
Perform the task “research prediction markets as a data source or product signal” for [target system/scenario].
1. Clarify goal, scope, baseline information, and success criteria; list missing context.
2. Study only relevant materials and assess sources and quote quality, market-implied probability, liquidity/biases, integration limitations, and the absence of investment recommendations.
3. Present conclusions with sources, methodology, and confidence; do not fill gaps with invented numbers.
4. Check conclusions against criteria and cite evidence, assumptions, and unknowns.

Constraints: Use substantiated sources, distinguish facts from estimates, do not provide investment advice or take external action without approval.
Use output format [expected format]. Do not invent facts; stop and request approval before any unplanned side effect.
```
